How it works

  1. Sign up with your name, email and a password — we email you a verification code once, to confirm the email is yours. After that you simply log in with your email and password.
  2. Upload your CV — we read your skills from it automatically.
  3. See matched jobs from all over Pakistan, with a match % for each job.
  4. Apply your way: click to apply yourself, or turn on auto-apply and email-applications go out from your own connected email.
  5. Portal jobs (Indeed, LinkedIn, Rozee forms) are always applied by you — we never ask for your portal passwords.

Auto-apply

When auto-apply is ON, matching jobs that accept applications by email are applied for you automatically, from your connected email, with your CV attached. Each job is applied only once, ever. Jobs on portals (Indeed, LinkedIn, Rozee forms) are never auto-applied — those you always apply to yourself.
